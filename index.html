Establishment

A Property of Unrivalled Character

Nestled on the storied Hardturmstrasse in the heart of Zurich, our establishment offers an unparalleled residential experience for the discerning flatmate. We combine the intimacy of shared living with the chaos of a five-star property managed by no one in particular.

Our team of world-class residents is on call 24 hours a day — primarily to ask if you've seen the good pan — and our kitchen operates as a rotating gallery of culinary experiments, some intentional.

Whether you're seeking a quiet evening in or a spirited debate about whose turn it is to buy dish soap, Hotel Hardturmstrasse delivers.

Guest Charter

The Rules of the Establishment

  • Wi-Fi credentials shall be shared freely. Any change to the password must be announced with minimum 72 hours notice and a formal written apology.
  • The good pan is communal property. It may not be used for non-stick purposes by those who have lost non-stick privileges.
  • Dishes left in the sink enter a 48-hour grace period. After that, they belong to the hotel and will be displayed in the lobby as art.
  • Bathroom karaoke is permitted between 08:00 and 22:00. Encores require a majority vote.
  • Any food labelled with a name shall be respected. Food unlabelled for more than 7 days becomes common property. Food unlabelled for 14 days becomes a biohazard.
  • All guests are encouraged to complete the dish soap procurement rota in a timely and equitable manner. Or just buy some, it's like 2 francs.
  • Profound statements made after midnight are to be collected and submitted to the guestbook. It is a civic duty.
